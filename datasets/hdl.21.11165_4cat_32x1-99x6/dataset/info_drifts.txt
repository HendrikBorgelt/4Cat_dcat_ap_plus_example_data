The DRIFT spectra have been converted to pseudo absorbance from reflectance according to:
Anal. Chem. 2007, 79, 10, 3912–3918	DOI: 10.1021/ac0702802
pseudo absorbance = log(1/reflectance)
The data was also baseline corrected to have the spectra stacked. OriginPro 2023 was used for the analyses and plotting.
